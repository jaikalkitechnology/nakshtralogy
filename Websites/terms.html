C3. Earnings, platform fee & taxation of payouts

Astrologers are independent consultants appointed by the Company to provide consultations to Users. In consideration of these services, the Company pays each Astrologer a consulting fee ("Payout"), calculated on the per-minute rate the Astrologer sets, less the Company's disclosed platform/facilitation fee. This clause explains, at a general level, how Indian tax law applies to that Payout. It is provided for transparency and does not replace independent tax advice.

  • You set your per-minute rates within Platform guidelines. The Company charges a platform/facilitation fee, disclosed in the astrologer console; the remainder is your Payout.
  • Payouts accrue to your Platform ledger and are settled to your registered bank account per the payout schedule (subject to a minimum payout threshold shown in the console).
  • The Company may withhold or reverse amounts relating to refunds, chargebacks, fraud, or verified complaints, and may adjust future payouts accordingly.

C3.1 Nature of income. Because an Astrologer is an independent consultant and not an employee, the Payout is not "salary." It is business/professional income in your own hands, taxable under "Profits and Gains of Business or Profession" under the Income-tax Act, 1961 (consolidated into the Income-tax Act, 2025 with effect from 1 April 2026). You are individually responsible for computing and paying your own income tax and filing your own return; the Company's role is limited to deducting tax at source where required and reporting that deduction.

C3.2 Tax Deducted at Source (TDS).

ProvisionSection 194J, Income-tax Act, 1961 (professional/technical services) — consolidated as Section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 from 1 April 2026 (rates/thresholds unchanged).
Rate10% on professional fees (astrology consulting treated as professional services).
ThresholdTDS applies once aggregate Payouts to you in the financial year exceed ₹50,000 (raised from ₹30,000 w.e.f. 1 April 2025).
No / invalid PANTDS is deducted at 20% under Section 206AA instead of 10%.
Non-residentSection 195 applies instead of 194J — at Act/DTAA rates, with Form 15CA/15CB compliance before remittance.
ReportingThe Company issues Form 16A quarterly and files Form 26Q; you can verify the credit in your Form 26AS / AIS.

C3.3 GST & TCS. If your aggregate turnover of taxable services exceeds ₹20 lakh in a financial year (₹10 lakh in specified special-category states), you must register under the CGST Act, 2017 and charge 18% GST on the fee you invoice the Company. Astrology consultation is not, at present, notified under Section 9(5) of the CGST Act, so GST liability (where applicable) rests with the registered Astrologer. As an e-commerce operator the Company collects TCS at 0.5% (Section 52) on the net taxable value of services supplied by each registered Astrologer, deposits it monthly, files Form GSTR-8, and issues a TCS certificate you can credit in your electronic cash ledger. The platform fee the Company retains is itself a taxable supply attracting 18% GST.

C3.4 Your own compliance. File an Income Tax Return declaring Payouts as business/professional income after claiming TDS credit; pay advance tax if net tax payable exceeds ₹10,000; maintain records under Section 44AA (or evaluate presumptive taxation under Section 44ADA with your CA); if GST-registered, issue tax invoices and file GSTR-1/3B; and assess any state Professional Tax obligation.
