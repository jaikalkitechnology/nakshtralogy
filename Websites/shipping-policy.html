★ In short: Nakshatralogy is a 100% digital service. Everything you buy is delivered electronically, instantly or in real time — there is no courier, tracking number, shipping charge, or physical delivery involved.

1. No physical shipment

Nakshatralogy is a fully digital platform. We do not manufacture, sell, warehouse, or ship any physical goods. There is no courier, logistics partner, tracking number, shipping charge, or physical delivery address involved in using the Service. Any reference elsewhere (for example, in payment-gateway paperwork) to "shipping" should be read as referring to the digital delivery described below.

3. If a digital product fails to deliver

If a paid report fails to generate, a purchased entitlement does not activate, or a consultation cannot start due to a technical fault on our side, this is treated as a delivery failure. Please contact support within 48 hours — you are entitled to a regenerated report, a working entitlement, or a refund of the affected amount, as set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

4. No shipping charges

Because nothing is physically shipped, no shipping, handling, or courier charge is, or will be, added to any order. Any price shown at checkout is the full price payable for the digital product or service, inclusive of applicable GST for Indian users.

5. No physical products are sold

The Platform does not sell, dispatch, or fulfil any physical item — including printed reports, gemstones, yantras, pooja kits, or remedy kits.

⚠ Remedies are informational only. Astrologers on the Platform may suggest remedies as guidance, but no astrologer or third party may sell, take payment for, or ship any physical product through the Platform. Nakshatralogy neither collects payment for, nor delivers, any physical item. Any physical purchase you choose to make elsewhere on an astrologer's suggestion is entirely between you and that external seller, outside this Platform and this Policy.

Should the Company ever decide to offer a physical item in future, we will first publish a dedicated courier-based Shipping Policy — covering the logistics partner, serviceable pin codes, dispatch and delivery timelines, shipping charges, order tracking, and the process for damaged, lost, or undelivered shipments, consistent with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 — before any such product is made available. Until then, no physical item is available for purchase on the Platform.
