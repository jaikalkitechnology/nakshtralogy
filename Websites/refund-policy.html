Refund & Cancellation Policy

EFFECTIVE: 20 JAN 2025 · LAST UPDATED: 2 AUG 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy is issued by Jaikalki Technology Private Limited (CIN U62091MH2025PTC438336), operator of the Nakshatralogy platform, and forms part of our Terms & Conditions. It explains when and how you can obtain a refund or cancel across wallet, consultations, paid reports, subscriptions, international in-app purchases, and the Developer API.

★ Our principle: you should pay only for value you actually receive. Where a technical failure on our side or an eligible service issue occurs, we refund promptly. Because astrology reports and completed consultations are delivered digitally and consumed immediately, they are generally non-refundable once delivered — the specific rules are below.

Consultation refunds

  • Astrologer no-show or technical drop: if a session cannot start or is interrupted by a platform/network fault, the charged minutes for the failed portion are auto-credited or refunded on review.
  • Quality concerns: if an astrologer behaved unprofessionally (per our Terms), raise it within 48 hours; we may refund part or all of the session at our discretion and act against the astrologer.
  • Dissatisfaction with predictions alone is not, by itself, grounds for a refund, since astrology is guidance and not a guaranteed outcome.

International purchases (App Store / Google Play)

For international users, paid items are sold as in-app purchases through the Apple App Store and Google Play. These are governed by the store's refund rules; refund requests for store purchases must typically be made to Apple or Google directly. Where a purchase grants a Platform entitlement that failed to deliver, contact us and we will help reconcile it.

How to request a refund

1

Contact support within 48 hours of the transaction via in-app Help or support@jaikalkitechnology.com.
2
Review
We verify the transaction, session logs, and eligibility. We may ask for your order ID and details.
3

We confirm the outcome — full refund, partial refund, wallet credit, or a reasoned decline — within 5–7 business days.
4
Settle
Approved refunds reach your source account in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank/gateway.

Non-refundable items

  • Consultation minutes already delivered satisfactorily;
  • Reports/forecasts already generated and delivered (absent a genuine defect);
  • Subscription periods where benefits or the report allowance have been used;
  • Developer API usage already metered and served;
  • Amounts flagged for fraud, abuse, or chargeback misuse.
⚠ Chargebacks: please contact us before raising a bank chargeback. Chargebacks on legitimately delivered digital services may lead to account suspension pending resolution.

For astrologers

When a user is refunded for an eligible failed or disputed session, the corresponding earning may be reversed or withheld from your ledger. Verified quality complaints can affect payouts and standing per the astrologer terms.
