Refund & Cancellation Policy

EFFECTIVE: 20 JAN 2025 · LAST UPDATED: 8 AUG 2026 (RESTATED)

This Refund & Cancellation Policy is issued by Jaikalki Technology Private Limited (CIN U62091MH2025PTC438336), operator of the Nakshatralogy platform, and forms part of our Terms & Conditions. It explains when and how you can obtain a refund or cancel across wallet, consultations, paid reports, subscriptions, international in-app purchases, and the Developer API.

★ Our principle: you should pay only for value you actually receive. Where a technical failure on our side or an eligible service issue occurs, we refund promptly. Because astrology reports and completed consultations are delivered digitally and consumed immediately, they are generally non-refundable once delivered — the specific rules are below.

Wallet refunds

The Nakshatralogy Wallet is prepaid platform credit usable only within Nakshatralogy — it is not a bank deposit, e-money, or a prepaid payment instrument (see Terms A2A).

  • Refunds for failed, rejected, returned, or cancelled transactions are credited back to your Wallet promptly.
  • Where a refund cannot be credited to your Wallet (for example, on account closure), it is credited to the original payment source — bank account, UPI, or card — used for the top-up.
  • Wallet credit cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to another user. Unused credit that you actually paid for can be refunded to your original source on request; bonus or promotional credit cannot.
  • No interest is payable on Wallet balances.

GST on refunds

Where a GST-inclusive payment is refunded (in full or in part), the Company issues a credit note under Section 34 of the CGST Act, 2017, adjusting the GST already reported on that transaction. You do not need to separately claim a GST refund from the tax department for Platform-billed transactions — the adjustment happens through our GST returns and is reflected in your invoice history in-app.

Consultation refunds

  • Astrologer no-show or technical drop: if a session cannot start or is interrupted by a platform/network fault, the charged minutes for the failed portion are auto-credited or refunded on review.
  • Quality concerns: if an astrologer behaved unprofessionally (per our Terms), raise it within 48 hours; we may refund part or all of the session at our discretion and act against the astrologer.
  • Dissatisfaction with predictions alone is not, by itself, grounds for a refund, since astrology is guidance and not a guaranteed outcome.
⚠ Only on-Platform payments are protected. Nakshatralogy will never ask you to pay an astrologer directly. Pay only through the app's official wallet/checkout — never send money, UPI, cash, gift cards, or bank transfers to an astrologer's personal account, even for a "special pooja" or to "remove a dosha". Payments made outside the Platform are not covered by this Policy and cannot be refunded or mediated by the Company. If an astrologer asks for off-Platform payment, do not pay and report them via Customer Support.

International purchases (App Store / Google Play)

For international users, paid items are sold as in-app purchases through the Apple App Store and Google Play. These are governed by the store's own published refund windows and process, which are independent of — and may differ from — the Company's 48-hour reporting window and 5–7 business-day decision timeline described below (those apply only to transactions billed directly by the Platform, e.g. via Razorpay for Indian users). Refund requests for store purchases must typically be made to Apple or Google directly. Where a purchase grants a Platform entitlement that failed to deliver, contact us and we will help reconcile it.

How to request a refund

We verify the transaction, session logs, and eligibility. We may ask for your order ID and details.
3

We confirm the outcome — full refund, partial refund, wallet credit, or a reasoned decline — within 5–7 business days.
4

Approved refunds reach your source account in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank/gateway.

Non-refundable items

  • Consultation minutes already delivered satisfactorily;
  • Reports/forecasts already generated and delivered (absent a genuine defect);
  • Subscription periods where benefits or the report allowance have been used;
  • Developer API usage already metered and served;
  • Amounts flagged for fraud, abuse, or chargeback misuse.
⚠ Chargebacks: please contact us before raising a bank chargeback. Chargebacks on legitimately delivered digital services may lead to account suspension pending resolution.

For astrologers

When a user is refunded for an eligible failed or disputed session, the corresponding earning may be reversed or withheld from your ledger. Verified quality complaints can affect payouts and standing per the astrologer terms.

If you disagree with a refund decision

  • Ask us to review — reply to your original support ticket or email support@jaikalki.com with any new information.
  • Escalate to our Grievance Officer — Krunal Trivedi, krunal@jaikalki.com (acknowledged within 48 hours, resolution endeavoured within 30 days).
  • Grievance Appellate Committee — appeal to the GAC constituted under the IT Rules, 2021 (gac.gov.in) for intermediary-related grievances.
  • Consumer forum — the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000 / 1915) or the e-Daakhil portal (edaakhil.nic.in), or the consumer commission of competent jurisdiction, without prejudice to the above.

Record-keeping

We retain transaction, refund, and communication records for the periods required under the Income-tax Act, the CGST Act, and our Privacy Policy's retention schedule, to support audits and any dispute resolution.
