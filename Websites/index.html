Common Questions

FAQ & answers

Is Nakshtralogy free to use?
Yes — generating your Kundli, viewing your daily horoscope, and exploring our 16 AI-powered birth chart engines are completely free. Live consultations with certified astrologers are charged per minute, with rates clearly visible before you start any session.
How accurate is the Vedic Kundli generated by Nakshtralogy?
Our Kundli engine uses Lahiri Ayanamsa with Swiss Ephemeris and NASA-grade astronomical data. Each prediction is validated across D-1 (Rasi), D-9 (Navamsa), D-10 (Dasamsa), D-24 (Siddhamsa), and D-60 (Shashtyamsa) divisional charts. Every report includes a transparent confidence score so you know exactly how reliable each insight is.
Can astrologers earn on Nakshtralogy?
Yes. Certified Vedic astrologers can register on the dedicated Astrologer App, set their per-minute rates for chat, voice, and video consultations, and earn 80% of the consultation fee. Top astrologers earn ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000+ per month. Registration takes 48 hours after verification.
What life dimensions does Nakshtralogy analyze?
We provide deep analysis across 16 life dimensions: Career & Profession, Business & Startup, Education & Learning, Wealth & Finance, Debt & Recovery, Love & Romance, Marriage & Family, Health & Wellness, Mental & Stress, Property & Assets, Legal & Disputes, Life Path & Luck, Character Analysis, Past Life & Karma, Kundli Milan (compatibility), and Relationship Intelligence (multi-person dynamics).
How does Kundli Milan / marriage matching work?
Our Kundli Milan engine performs traditional Ashtakoot (8-Gun) matching across Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana, Bhakoot, and Nadi — plus modern checks for Mangal Dosh, Papasamya, D-9 Navamsa cross, Venus-Mars chemistry, and joint dasha alignment. The result: a 186-key compatibility report covering 9 domains.
Is my birth data private?
Absolutely. Your birth details and chart data are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your data with third parties. Live consultations are end-to-end encrypted, and you can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app.
What languages are supported?
The app interface is available in English, Hindi, and Marathi. Astrologer consultations are available in 12+ Indian languages including Hindi, English, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, and Sanskrit. Filter astrologers by language when browsing.
Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?
Yes. We have a transparent refund policy — if a consultation experience falls short, contact our support team within 48 hours and we'll review and refund eligible charges. Read our complete Refund Policy for details.
