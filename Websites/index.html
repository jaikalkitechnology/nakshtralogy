Common Questions

FAQ & answers

What is Nakshatralogy?
Nakshatralogy is a Vedic astrology platform by Jaikalki Technology Private Limited. It generates a free Vedic Kundli (birth chart), delivers AI-powered reports across 16 life dimensions, and connects you with certified astrologers over live chat, voice, and video. It runs on Android, iOS, and the web at app.nakshatralogy.com.
Is Nakshatralogy free to use?
Yes. Generating your Kundli, viewing your daily horoscope, and exploring your AI-powered birth-chart insights are free. Live consultations with certified astrologers are charged per minute, with the rate shown before you start any session. Some deep specialist reports and premium features are paid — always with the price displayed upfront.
How accurate is the Vedic Kundli generated by Nakshatralogy?
The Kundli engine uses Lahiri Ayanamsa with Swiss Ephemeris (NASA-grade astronomical data) and validates every reading across the D-1 (Rasi), D-9 (Navamsa), D-10 (Dasamsa), D-24 (Siddhamsa), and D-60 (Shashtyamsa) divisional charts. Every report carries a transparent confidence score so you know how reliable each insight is.
How do I talk to an astrologer on Nakshatralogy?
Open the Nakshatralogy app or app.nakshatralogy.com, browse 5,000+ verified astrologers, filter by speciality, language, and price, add money to your wallet, and start a chat, voice, or video consultation. Billing is per minute and fully transparent.
Can astrologers earn on Nakshatralogy?
Yes. Certified Vedic astrologers can register on the dedicated astrologer app or at astro.nakshatralogy.com, set their own per-minute rates for chat, voice, and video, and keep the majority of each consultation fee. The console provides real-time earnings, instant payouts, a client-chart library, and analytics. Verification typically completes within 48 hours.
What life dimensions does Nakshatralogy analyze?
Nakshatralogy analyzes 16 life dimensions: Career & Profession, Business & Startup, Education & Learning, Wealth & Finance, Debt & Recovery, Love & Romance, Marriage & Family, Health & Wellness, Mental & Stress, Property & Assets, Legal & Disputes, Life Path & Luck, Character Analysis, Past Life & Karma, Kundli Milan (compatibility), and Relationship Intelligence.
How does Kundli Milan / marriage matching work?
Kundli Milan performs traditional Ashtakoot (8-Gun) matching across Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana, Bhakoot, and Nadi — plus modern checks for Mangal Dosh, Papasamya, D-9 Navamsa cross, Venus-Mars chemistry, and joint dasha alignment, producing a detailed compatibility report across nine domains.
Does Nakshatralogy have a developer astrology API?
Yes. Developers can sign up at developer.nakshatralogy.com for the Nakshatralogy Developer API — REST endpoints for Kundli, the 16 specialist reports, Kundli Milan, dashas, transits, and today's forecast. One monthly All-Access plan covers every product, is funded from a wallet, and is rate-limited to 1 request per second.
Is Nakshatralogy available outside India?
Yes. Nakshatralogy supports international users with region detection, a dozen international languages (English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Indonesian), right-to-left layout for Arabic, and USD pricing via the App Store and Google Play. India uses the wallet flow in INR.
Is my birth data private and secure?
Yes. Your birth details and chart data are encrypted in transit and at rest. Nakshatralogy never sells your data. Live consultations are private, and you can delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?
Yes, subject to our policy. If a consultation or paid report falls short due to a technical failure or eligible issue, contact support within 48 hours and the charge will be reviewed and refunded where eligible. Read our complete Refund Policy for details.
