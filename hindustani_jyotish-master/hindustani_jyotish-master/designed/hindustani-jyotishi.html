Design Rationale

Why it works

Canvas Philosophy
Deep indigo, not yellow
The old Jyotishi app mixed bright yellow headers with cosmic-dark calculators — two clashing worlds. Hindustani Jyotishi commits fully to a #0B0B1E indigo void everywhere, so the whole app feels like one continuous night sky. Premium, calm, mystical.
Two-App Distinction
User vs Astrologer
Both apps share the indigo canvas and diya logo, but the User app leans saffron (action, seeking) while the Astrologer app leans copper (craft, profession). Earnings & "you earned" states use green so money always reads positive.
Saffron = Action
One sacred accent
Saffron #F4811F is reserved strictly for primary actions — Sign Up, Send OTP, Recharge, Start Chat. It never decorates. This makes every screen's "what do I tap" instantly obvious, and ties to the sacred saffron of Indian tradition.
Gold = Premium
Ratings & cosmic
Gold #E8B547 appears only for ratings (★ 5.0), wallet balances, kundli charts, and paid insight prices. It signals "this is valuable / premium," distinct from saffron's "tap me."
Trust & Security
Biometric + Google
Both apps offer Google login and fingerprint/Face ID. The astrologer side makes biometric extra-prominent (filled saffron card) since they log in frequently throughout the day to attend requests.
Bilingual by Design
हिन्दुस्तानी roots
Devanagari (Noto Sans Devanagari) sits alongside the Fraunces display serif — नमस्ते, हिन्दी, ज्योतिषी appear naturally. The EN/हिं toggle is one tap. The name itself anchors the brand in Hindustani identity.
