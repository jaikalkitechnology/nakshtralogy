Layout · 5 Groups
Grouped, not listed
16 tiles in a flat grid feels overwhelming. Instead, the screen groups them into 5 thematic sections: Career & Purpose (4), Wealth & Assets (4), Love & Relationships (4), Health & Wellness (2), Soul & Karma (2). Each group gets a small mono-spaced label header. The eye lands on a group at a time.
Design · Color-coded
Each engine, its own gradient
Every tile uses a different gradient pulled from the cosmic palette: gold for Career, saffron for Business, teal-mint for Education, sky-blue for Mental, rose for Marriage, lavender for Past Life. Users learn the color language fast — soon they'll recognize "the gold one" or "the rose one" without reading labels.
Tile · Anatomy
Five data points, one tap
Each tile shows: engine #, colored icon, title with em-italic, keys/sections count, core score, and a status pill (FRESH / UPDATED / NEW). The premium ★ ribbon flags the deepest engines (Marriage, Life Path, Past Life, Kundli Milan, Relationship Intel).
Filter · Pills
Quick narrowing
Filter pills at the top let users jump to a category: All · 16, Career · 3, Wealth · 3, Love · 4, Health · 2, Soul · 2. Combined with the visual groups below, this gives both scan and search entry points.
Hero · Banner
Set the scope
The top hero banner shows 16 ENGINES · 2,000+ DATA POINTS · 12 FRESH — establishing the depth of analysis available before the user starts tapping. The "FRESH" stat hints at when reports update so users don't worry about stale data.
CTA · AI Council
Single discuss entry
A persistent CTA at the bottom — "Discuss with AI Council · ChatGPT · Claude · Gemini" — lets users open a meta-conversation across ALL their reports, not just one. The dashed gold border signals it's special / different from regular tiles.
Status · Pills
Three signals
FRESH (green) = report generated recently. UPDATED (amber) = chart data changed, regenerate recommended. NEW (gold gradient) = engine just released, user hasn't tapped yet. The ★ ribbon on premium tiles is for deepest-content engines — marketed as "longest reading."
Tab · Insights
New nav slot
The bottom nav now has HOME · KUNDLI · INSIGHTS · CHAT · PROFILE. The Insights tab (active here) replaces the previous "Live" or service-list tab. This makes the engine hub a first-class destination, not buried inside Kundli detail.
