Engine 03 · Education Mode
Student-grade clarity
Career was about "what role", Business was "what venture", Education is about "what subject, how to study, when to push." The 15 sections give a student concrete answers: top 3 subjects, best study windows, ideal learning environment, and the bridge from education to career.
Section · Three Houses
4th + 5th + 9th side-by-side
Education needs three houses, not one or two. 4th = primary education foundation, 5th = intelligence (highlighted as featured), 9th = higher learning & research. The triple-card layout keeps all three visible at once with each house's strength score and lord placement.
Section · D-24 Highlighted
Siddhamsa is the chart
For education, D-24 Siddhamsa is the most important divisional chart — it shows learning achievement specifically. The tab gets a ★ marker and is selected by default. Other engines highlight different divisionals (Career → D-10, Business → D-11, Marriage → D-9).
Section · Subject Ranking
12 subjects, one view
All 12 subjects ranked vertically with their score bars. Top 3 get gold-tinted background highlighting — this is the single most actionable section for a student trying to choose a stream. The horizontal bar gives instant visual comparison; the score gives precision.
Section · Degree Likelihood
Quick 2×2 reality check
formal_degree: 59.8 = green/medium, doctorate: 53.1 = orange/low, foreign_education: 36.5 = red/poor. Color-coded scores let a student see their realistic options at a glance — without the engine being judgmental about it.
Section · Challenges & Gifts
Twin balance cards
Side-by-side cards: amber-tinted Challenges (with concentration_difficulty & education_break_risk shown as low percentages = good news) + green-tinted Gifts (with cognitive_gifts and natural_abilities as pills). The visual symmetry tells students "here's what to watch + here's what to lean on."
